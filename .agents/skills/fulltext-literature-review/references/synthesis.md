# Concise synthesis

The coordinator writes synthesis.md while papers arrive, clearly marking the draft as pending. Use current paper evidence and actual check dispositions; no need to wait for perfect administrative records to start writing.

Follow the editorial brief in SKILL.md. Start the comparison table with the first returned paper and label unavailable rows as awaiting reading; replace these before final delivery. Draft method relationships during checks, then update only claims whose disposition changes. Freeze the final synthesis for its brief check after upstream checks settle.

Use this shape:
1. Research context and the question connecting the supplied papers.
2. Method motivations, mechanisms and contributions, with one compact comparison table.
3. Relationships and differences between approaches, distinguishing your synthesis from author claims.
4. Evidence scope and material limitations, paired with their implications for interpretation or further research.
5. Links to every paper report, followed by a References / 參考文獻 list mapping every reviewed paper ID to its academic citation.

## References

Default to APA 7; use IEEE when the user requests it. Preserve [P001], [P002], etc. as corpus cross-reference labels, not as substitutes for citations. Include every reviewed paper, including those with inconclusive results; do not import the papers' entire bibliographies. Use one consistent style and preserve the actual title and author order. For IEEE retain its normal numeric reference number and add the corpus ID for mapping.

Collect bibliographic details from supplied title pages and available metadata while building the comparison table: authors, year, exact title, venue or manuscript type, volume/issue/pages where applicable, and verified DOI/URL when available. Use linked local source files when no verified public identifier is available. Do not infer a venue, publication status or year from a method name, filename or familiar paper. For missing dates use APA n.d.; omit unknown venue/pages and briefly disclose missing metadata once. External metadata lookup is not required for ordinary delivery.

APA example with placeholders: [P001] Surname, A. A., & Surname, B. B. (Year). *Exact paper title*. Venue or supplied manuscript description. DOI/URL or local source link. Apply the style's author-list rule rather than automatically replacing the authors with et al. Bibliographic entries are outside the prose-length target and the academic/evidence balance. They require only a quick ID/title/author consistency check within the existing final check, not another review round.

Use neutral academic language: describe the tested setting, observed result and remaining question. Do not speculate about author motives or characterize work as inflated, misleading or defective without specific evidence. Present contributions fairly before discussing limits. Mention a limitation once where it matters, rather than repeating cautions in the introduction, table and conclusion. Keep necessary numerical qualifications; move detailed discrepancy logs to the linked paper/check records. Do not add an audit-style ending to an academic review.

Aim for about 800–1,500 Chinese characters, adjusting to the question. Avoid repeating the table in prose. Distinguish source findings from your cross-paper interpretation. Different tasks, datasets and metrics do not support a common ranking or pooled effect.

Unverified evidence may be discussed only as provisional, with affected conclusions visibly qualified. If a central question cannot be answered, say why. The final artifact is always synthesis.md, including partial delivery; do not substitute a test-status report for literature synthesis.

Use a different agent for one brief synthesis check. Address concrete findings or state limits, then deliver the report. Keep runtime diagnostics and timing in run.md, not the literature discussion.
